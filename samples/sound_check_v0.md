# Sound check v0

**Purpose:** read these aloud and decide whether this is the sound you want.
**Nothing here is final.** The words are placeholders picked from `dammaz.wordgen`
output to show the sound; the real lexicon comes in Phase 3. The grammar is the
Romance-lite draft from `PLAN.md` Phase 2.

## How to read it

- **Stress the first syllable of every root**, never a suffix:
  DRASH-ad, KA-rak, UN-guz, SKRE-kan.
- Vowels are always "pure" and never silent:
  `a` as in *father*, `e` as in *bed*, `i` as in *machine*, `o` as in *more*,
  `u` as in *rule*.
- `g` is always hard (*go*), `s` is always voiceless (*hiss*), `z` is always buzzed
  (*zoo*), and `r` is rolled or tapped.
- Digraphs are single sounds:

  | Digraph | Sound |
  |---|---|
  | `kh` | *loch* |
  | `zh` | *measure* |
  | `sh` | *ship* |
  | `th` | *thin* |
  | `dh` | *this* |
  | `ng` | *sing* (never *n+g*) |

## Sentences

**1.** *The old dwarf forged a strong axe in the mountain.*
```
Ta dawi dhal drashad un az krang bin ta karak.
```

## Draft rules used

| | Rule | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Plural | `-i` after a consonant, `-n` after a vowel | `az → azi`, `dawi → dawin`, `skreka → skrekan` |
| Present | bare root (same as infinitive and imperative), including the copula `zu` | `Or glok bral` |
| Past | `-ad` | `drashad` |
| Progressive | `-en` | `khurken` |
| Future | `an` + root | `an unguz` |
| Imperative | bare root | `truk!` |
| Negation | `nai` before the verb | `nai kend` |
| Question | `wan` at the start, normal word order | `Wan uz thiz ta gleth?` |
| Possessive | pronoun + `-a`, before the noun | `ora` my, `uza` your, `eka` his/her/its, `uta` our, `afa` your (pl), `uma` their |
| Derivation | `-ki` person, `-az` place, `-ul` craft, `-ak` quality | `drashki` smith, `zidaz` here, `drashul` smithcraft, `krangak` strength |

## Outcome (2026-09-27)

1. **Sound approved.** The phonology in `spec/phonology.md` and the `style` knobs in
   `phonology.yaml` are the baseline going forward.
2. **Present tense is the bare root.** The earlier draft used `-it` on every
   present-tense verb (`Or glokit bral`), which got repetitive. Since `-it` is no
   longer a suffix, roots may now end in `-it`.
3. **Inflected-form checks accepted.** The plural of `khaz` is `khazi` (British
   slang for a toilet). The Phase 3 lexicon validator will check inflected and
   derived forms against English and the profanity lists, not just roots.
